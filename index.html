About Me

I’m Christian Daniel Jensen, and my life’s work is the Continuum Universes — a vast, interconnected multiverse where stories, games, and lore evolve as one. Every creation I touch — a game mechanic, a novel, a wiki entry, a custom-built system — exists to expand this ever-growing universe.

Jensen Saga Studio is my creative empire, built to turn storytelling into experience — through games, immersive lore, and dynamic, evolving universes. All of it hands-on by design: AI tooling accelerates my pipeline; it never authors the canon. I’m early-career in professional web development, but I ship real things: custom MediaWiki installations, self-hosted infrastructure, and homegrown tooling — including custom MCP integrations that automate my card-production pipeline.

The Road So Far

I built Jensen Saga Studio not just as a creative outlet, but as a way forward — out of poverty, out of disability, out of being trapped in a system that doesn’t value creative minds.

I’m neurodivergent, and I fight through Bipolar Depression, along with the financial struggles and setbacks that come with it. For years I battled with my health — the named fight and the invisible ones — bouncing between jobs I couldn’t sustain, stuck in a cycle that felt impossible to break.

I refuse to stay in survival mode forever. Jensen Saga Studio is my path to self-sufficiency: through my games, my stories, and my worlds, I’m building something that will support me, outlive me, and inspire others who feel just as trapped as I once did. To the other neurodivergent builders out there: you are not broken, and you are not alone. Build anyway. Creativity isn’t just my passion — it’s my lifeline.
